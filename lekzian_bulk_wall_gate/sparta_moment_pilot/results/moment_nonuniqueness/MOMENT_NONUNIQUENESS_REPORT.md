# Constructive moment non-uniqueness

Two non-negative discrete velocity distributions were constructed for each wall functional. Every full-range monomial moment through total degree three is identical, while the incident half-range wall functional differs.

| Functional | f− | f+ | Difference | Maximum moment mismatch | Minimum probability |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Pressure | 0.958723 | 1.034560 | 0.075837 | 6.349e-16 | 8.279e-10 |
| Shear | -0.021644 | 0.021644 | 0.043289 | 2.229e-16 | 8.279e-10 |

This establishes pointwise non-uniqueness for the finite full-range S0/S1/S2 moment hierarchy. It does not by itself prove that every finite spatial patch is non-identifying; the DSMC gate tests that practical question empirically.
